KEY FINDINGS
------------
1. Single-stream decode (b=1) is SGLang's from TP4 up: 5.90 ms at TP4 (-15% vs vLLM),
   6.36 ms at TP8 (-7%). SGLang sweet spot is TP4; it regresses TP4->TP8 while vLLM
   improves monotonically (7.32 -> 6.99 -> 6.82 ms). At TP2 vLLM won b=1.
2. Throughput at any batch (b>=16) is vLLM's decisively: at TP8 +52% (b=16), +52% (b=64),
   +67% (b=128). Peak of sweep: vLLM TP8 b=128 = 8219 tok/s.
3. vLLM TP4 (half the GPUs) out-throughputs SGLang TP8 at b=128: 6394 vs 4914 tok/s.
4. Tail consistency: vLLM p99 TPOT within 0.2-3% of mean in every cell; SGLang +6% to +41%.
5. TTFT: vLLM leads all TP4 cells; at TP8 SGLang wins b=64 (127 vs 202 ms) and b=128
   (147 vs 175 ms) despite prefill CUDA graphs disabled (recipe requirement of
   --enable-decoder-swa-bounded-replay).
6. TP4->TP8 throughput gain: vLLM +13/24/29% (b=16/64/128); SGLang +22/16% at b=64/128,
   -6% at b=16.

PRACTICAL PICKS
---------------
- Latency-critical single-stream: SGLang TP4 (5.90 ms TPOT, 169 tok/s single stream).
- Throughput / batch serving: vLLM TP8 (8219 tok/s at b=128), TP4 for efficiency per GPU.

NOTES

  graph-covered (fairness); recipe otherwise identical for both frameworks.
